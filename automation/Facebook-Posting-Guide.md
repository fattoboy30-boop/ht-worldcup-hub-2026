## Engagement Tips

### Do's
✅ Ask questions to encourage comments  
✅ Use emojis to make posts stand out  
✅ Post at consistent times  
✅ Respond to comments quickly  
✅ Share user-generated content  
✅ Use polls and debates  
✅ Celebrate local connections  

### Don'ts
❌ Don't post too many links (Facebook suppresses them)  
❌ Don't use all caps (looks spammy)  
❌ Don't ignore comments  
❌ Don't post without checking facts  
❌ Don't forget the HT branding

## Facebook Page Best Practices

1. **Post 3-4 times daily** (not more, not less)
2. **Respond to all comments** within 1 hour
3. **Use high-quality images** (1200x630px recommended)
4. **Write short captions** (40-80 characters get most engagement)
5. **Include calls-to-action** (Comment, Share, Vote)
6. **Tag relevant pages** when appropriate
7. **Use Facebook Insights** to track best times
8. **Go live** for big matches
